# Epistemological Boundary & Evidence Framework | 四層認識論邊界憲章

### English
The Veracity strictly separates historical verification into four distinct layers. Cross-layer causal inference is formally prohibited:

| Layer | Question | Responsible Agent | System Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Layer 4: Interpretation** | What does it mean? What political motive lies behind it? | Human Historians | **Deterministic algorithms are strictly prohibited from adjudication** |
| **Layer 3: Authenticity** | Is it the document claimed? Is the series sequential? | Archival Principle of Provenance | System provides **heuristic exploration clues only** |
| **Layer 2: Provenance** | How was it digitized? When was it unsealed? | Custodial Chain & PREMIS Events | Ingests and structures official archive metadata |
| **Layer 1: Fixity** | Has the binary bitstream mutated since ingestion? | Cryptographic Hash & Signatures | **Core Engineering Guarantee** (SHA-256 / Sigstore) |

> **Cardinal Principle**: Veracity is not a binary output generated by algorithms, but an audit trail to assist human scholars in reaching their own historical judgments.

---

### 繁體中文
本專案將歷史數位資料之驗證嚴格劃分為四個獨立層次，嚴禁跨層進行因果推論：

| 層級 | 核心提問 | 負責主體 | 系統能力邊界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Layer 4: 歷史解釋 (Interpretation)** | 文字意圖為何？背後政治動機何在？ | 人類歷史學家 | **確定性代碼絕對禁止介入進行歷史裁判** |
| **Layer 3: 史料真確性 (Authenticity)** | 是否為聲稱的公文？編號是否連續？ | 檔案學全宗原則 | 提供卷號斷層等**啟發式探索線索** |
| **Layer 2: 來源與託管 (Provenance)** | 經何管道數位化？何時解密公諸於世？ | 託管鏈與 PREMIS 事件 | 記錄檔案館移交與擷取之結構化事件 |
| **Layer 1: 位元完整性 (Fixity)** | 此二進位流自取出後是否發生改變？ | 密碼學哈希與簽章 | **核心工程保障**（SHA-256 + Sigstore） |

> **核心原則**：Veracity（求真）不是本庫輸出的二元判決，而是本庫協助人類學者抵達歷史判斷時所依託的審計鏈。
