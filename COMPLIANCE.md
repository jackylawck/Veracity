## 1. Non-AI Scope Exclusion & Deterministic Nature Declaration
## 1. 非人工智慧系統範圍排除與確定性本質聲明

### English
**1.1 Exemption from AI Regulations (EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, CAC AI Provisions):**  
"The Veracity" is strictly an algorithmic, deterministic historical record ingestion, indexing, and cryptographic verification system. The architecture relies exclusively on deterministic cryptographic primitives (SHA-256, Ed25519, Sigstore Keyless transparency manifests) and structured relational schema mappings (Schema v2.0.0).  
* The system does **not** employ Large Language Models (LLMs), machine learning inferencing pipelines, generative artificial intelligence, or automated profiling algorithms.  
* Consequently, this project falls outside the statutory scope of the **European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**, **ISO/IEC 42001 (Artificial Intelligence Management System)**, and the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) **Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services**. Any regulatory requirements pertaining to high-risk AI, training data provenance for synthetic generation, or autonomous decision-making are hereby explicitly excluded as inapplicable.

### 中文
**1.1 人工智慧監管法規排除聲明（歐盟 AI Act、ISO/IEC 42001、國家網信辦生成式 AI 規範）：**  
「揭古（The Veracity）」本質上是確定性演算法驅動之歷史公文採集、索引與密碼學存證系統。底層完全依賴確定性密碼學原語（SHA-256、Ed25519、Sigstore Keyless 透明登錄簿）與結構化關聯元數據映射（Schema v2.0.0）。  
* 本系統**不包含**任何大型語言模型（LLM）、機器學習推理管線、生成式人工智慧代理或自動化畫像決策演算法。  
* 因此，本項目依法不屬於**歐盟《人工智慧法案》（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689）**、**ISO/IEC 42001（人工智慧管理體系）**及中國國家互聯網信息辦公室《生成式人工智能服務管理暫行辦法》所規範之範疇。所有關於高風險 AI 系統審查、生成式訓練數據合規性或演算法自主決策之法規義務，本項目均依法排除適用，無需承擔相關審批或申報。

## 2. Export Controls, ITAR / EAR & Dual-Use Exclusion
## 2. 出口管制、ITAR / EAR 與軍民兩用管制豁免聲明

### English
**2.1 Public Domain and Open Government Declassification Carve-Out:**  
All archival records, treaty identifiers, and dossier manifests indexed or cached by "The Veracity" are derived exclusively from officially declassified, publicly available records published by sovereign governments, international courts, or intergovernmental organizations (e.g., UK National Archives under the Public Record Act, US National Archives under FOIA and Mandatory Declassification Review, United Nations Treaty Series, ICJ Judgments).  
* **United States EAR Exclusion (15 C.F.R. § 734.3(b)(3)):** Under the Export Administration Regulations, information published or generally accessible to the interested public through public archives is categorized as "Publicly Available" and is not subject to the EAR.  
* **ITAR Exclusion (22 C.F.R. § 120.34(a)(1)):** Under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations, technical data in the "public domain" available through public libraries, open archives, and public releases is exempt.  
* **Dual-Use Regulation (EU Regulation 2021/821):** The project does not transfer, compile, or transmit controlled source code, cryptographic weapon systems, or non-public strategic industrial technologies.  
Therefore, the distribution, indexing, and publication of historical diplomatic and state dossiers through this platform comply with worldwide export control regimes and require no state export licensing.

### 中文
**2.2 公共領域與法定歷史解密豁免依據：**  
「揭古」所索引與錨定之全部公文、條約編號及全宗元數據，百分之百源自各國主權政府、國際司法法庭或多邊政府間國際組織依其法定解密年限程序（如英國《公共檔案法》、美國《資訊自由法》與強制解密審查程序、聯合國條約集、國際法院判決）正式開放予公眾查閱之公文。  
* **美國出口管制條例 EAR 豁免（15 C.F.R. § 734.3(b)(3)）：** 凡透過公共檔案館、圖書館向公眾普遍開放之資訊，法定歸類為「公開可用資訊（Publicly Available Information）」，不受 EAR 出口審查管制。  
* **美國國際武器貿易條例 ITAR 豁免（22 C.F.R. § 120.34(a)(1)）：** 公共領域（Public Domain）內之歷史技術公文，完全排除於受控防務資料範疇。  
* **歐盟軍民兩用管制（EU Regulation 2021/821）：** 本系統不轉移受控戰略技術、非公開軍用密碼工具或戰略軍民兩用製造圖紙。  
因此，本平台對歷史主權解密公文之索引與存證發佈，嚴格符合全球出口管制法規，毋須申請任何特許出口牌照。

## 3. Privacy, Data Protection & Historical Research Carve-Out
## 3. 個人隱私權、數據保護法規與歷史研究豁免

### English
**3.1 GDPR (EU), PDPO (HK Cap. 486), and PIPL (China) Archival Exemption:**  
The repository processes exclusively historical metadata and official government documentation. Personal data is handled strictly under the following statutory protections:  
* **Historical and Archival Purposes (GDPR Article 89(1)):** Personal data contained in historical state records is processed lawfully for archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes.  
* **Deceased Individuals Principle:** Under GDPR Recital 27 and standard international privacy doctrines, privacy regulations do not apply to the personal data of deceased persons. Given that "The Veracity" operates within a unified 30-year historical declassification window (1945–1996), named individuals in primary state dossiers are overwhelmingly historical public figures or deceased personalities.  
* **Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO Cap. 486, Section 62):** Personal data held solely for historical, archival, or research purposes is exempt from the Data Protection Principles regarding retention limits and correction demands, provided bits remain historical fact.  
* **China Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL Article 13):** Processing of information disclosed in accordance with state laws or official declassification regulations is permitted without supplementary consent.

### 中文
**3.1 歐盟 GDPR、香港《私隱條例》（第486章）及中國《個人信息保護法》之歷史檔案法定豁免：**  
本系統僅處理歷史公文目錄元數據與政府官方解密檔案，涉及人名之處理嚴格落實以下法理豁免：  
* **公共利益與歷史研究豁免（GDPR 第 89 條第 1 款）：** 為公共利益之歷史檔案保存與法證學術研究目的，依法豁免常規數據最小化與即時刪除要求。  
* **已故人士排除原則：** 依 GDPR 前言第 27 條及國際公認隱私法理，數據保護法規原則上不適用於已故人士。本系統實行「統一 30 年歷史解密窗口」（1945 年至 1996 年），所載人名絕大多數為冷戰歷史人物、外交官員或已故公眾人士。  
* **香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章第 62 條）：** 專為歷史、統計或研究目的而持有的個人資料，依法豁免受資料保留期限限制，亦不得藉此要求篡改歷史原案。  
* **中國《個人信息保護法》（第 13 條）：** 處理依照各國法律法規或政府法定解密程序已合法公開之個人資訊，毋須另行取得主體同意。

## 4. Cryptographic Provenance, Fixity & Layer-1 Non-Repudiation
## 4. 密碼學存證、位元不變性與 Layer-1 不可抵賴性聲明

### English
**4.1 Epistemological Boundary:**  
"The Veracity" guarantees **Layer-1 Bitstream Integrity (位元流完整性)** via SHA-256 and Sigstore/Cosign public transparency log registration (Rekor).  
* Cryptographic anchoring guarantees that the recorded document metadata has not suffered in-transit tampering, unauthorized post-facto modification, or digital bit rot since ingestion.  
* **No Historical Adjudication (非歷史實質裁判):** Cryptographic fixity does not assert the factual veracity, geopolitical morality, or substantive validity of the statements made by the original sovereign states within the primary documents. Sovereign claims, treaties, and intelligence briefs remain primary historical artifacts whose historiographical interpretation belongs exclusively to independent scholars and civil society.

### 中文
**4.1 認識論邊界與不可抵賴架構：**  
「揭古」透過 SHA-256 演算法與 Sigstore/Cosign 全球透明登記簿（Rekor）提供 **Layer-1 位元流完整性（Bitstream Integrity）**。  
* 密碼學存證僅擔保：本平台所記錄之公文元數據，自採集入庫起至用戶驗證時，絕無遭受中間人篡改、事後偽造或位元損毀。  
* **非歷史裁判聲明：** 密碼學固化並不代表對公文內文所述內容之真偽、地緣政治正當性或條約合法性進行實質裁判。各國主權宣示、情報評估及條約文本皆屬第一手原始史料，其歷史詮釋與評判權利永遠歸屬獨立歷史學者與國際社會。
